Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about switching your institution to Schoollyx.

How long does it take to migrate from our current software?

With Module #11 (Bulk Upload System), migrating student profiles, historical fee ledgers, and staff data takes under 48 hours. Our dedicated onboarding engineers assist your team with Excel / CSV validation and verification free of charge.

Can we choose only specific modules instead of all 38?

Yes! Schoollyx is completely modular. You can use our "My Package" builder to select only the modules your school needs right now (e.g. Fees, Attendance, and Parent App) and unlock other modules like LMS or Inventory whenever you wish.

How does the WhatsApp notification system work?

We connect directly via official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API. Parents receive branded alerts with payment links, bus delay notifications, and attendance check-in confirmations right on their WhatsApp chats with a 98% open rate.

Is student data secure and compliant?

Absolutely. All database records are encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We enforce granular role-based permissions and daily automated backups with 99.9% uptime SLA.
